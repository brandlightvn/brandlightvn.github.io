About us

ABOUT BRANDLIGHT

Brandlight Company Limited is a Vietnam-based Marketing & Creative Agency dedicated to helping brands build meaningful connections with Vietnamese consumers.

We combine local market expertise, strategic thinking, creative solutions, and strong execution capabilities to create marketing campaigns that are not only visually compelling, but also designed to deliver real business impact.

With a talented and experienced team across Hanoi, Da Nang, and Ho Chi Minh City, Brandlight provides brands with the local knowledge, network, and resources needed to successfully navigate the Vietnamese market.

MORE THAN A MARKETING AGENCY

We believe successful marketing starts with a deep understanding of the market. Every market has its own culture, consumer behavior, trends, and opportunities. That is why we do not simply adapt existing ideas to Vietnam. We develop solutions based on local insights and real consumer understanding.

From the first strategic idea to the final execution, Brandlight works as an extension of your team — helping brands understand, connect, engage, and grow in Vietnam.
